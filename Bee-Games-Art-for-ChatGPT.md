# Spelling Bee games: artwork prompts for ChatGPT

**How to use:** open a new ChatGPT chat. Copy **one** grey box, paste it, and send. Each box is complete on its own and makes one picture, so there's no setup step and nothing to combine. Download the picture and name it with the file name above its box.

**If ChatGPT gives a grey-and-white checkerboard instead of a see-through background:** that's a fake background drawn into the picture. Reply with: `Same picture, but on a plain solid white background.` I'll remove the white for you when I add it to the app.

**When you have them:** send me the images and I'll resize them and put them in the app. Each game shows an emoji until its picture arrives.
