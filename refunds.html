Refunds

Last updated 17 August 2026

There is a free week, unlocked, with no card. It's there so you can find out whether your child will actually use this before any money changes hands — please do use it.

The policy

No refunds once your key has been sent — but if it isn't working, tell me and I will either fix it or refund you. I would rather sort the problem than keep money from someone who couldn't use the thing.

If you cancel

You keep full access until the end of the year you have already paid for. Nothing cuts off part-way through a term. At the end of that year it stops renewing and the app locks the next time it is opened.

Cancel any time from the link in your purchase email — you don't have to ask me.

Charged twice, or something odd

Write to spellingquest@gmail.com with the receipt and I'll refund the duplicate the same day I see it.
